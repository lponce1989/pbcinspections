How we calculated this

  • One company, one vote. 16 companies appear across our city pages, many on several pages. County-wide medians and company counts count each company once; the county price range uses every published listing price, including per-city prices.
  • Per-city prices. Sun Inspections publishes a different price for each city. For the county-wide figures we use the median of its city prices. On the by-city table we use the price that company publishes for that city.
  • Which number. We use the stand-alone price the inspector lists, or its lowest "from" price. Where one company shows two figures for the same service on different pages (Accurate Building Inspections lists wind mitigation at $150 on its package page and $125 on its wind mitigation page), we use the figure on the page cited as the listing's source and note the other.
  • Size caps differ. Some prices cover homes up to 2,000, 2,500 or 3,000 sq ft; others state no cap. Larger, older or more distant homes often cost more. Each listing shows the cap.
  • Not counted: companies that publish no price for that service, and reports included "free" with a full home inspection (TruView, InspectionNow), because there is no stand-alone price.
  • "Firms publishing a price" = companies with a published price for that service / all listed companies.

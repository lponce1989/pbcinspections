Boca Raton wind mitigation, 4-point & roof certification inspectors

Contact inspectors directly: each listing below has a phone number, website and published prices. Jump to the list →

Boca Raton has roughly 49,000 housing units. The median home was built in 1983, and 43% were built before 1980 (ACS 2024 1-year estimates). With 43% of homes now over 45 years old, a 4-point inspection is a near-certain ask for owners applying to Citizens, which requires one for homes over 20 years old. Citizens also asks for a roof inspection once a shingle roof passes 25 years or a tile or metal roof passes 50; roof age, not house age, decides that one.

Wind-borne debris region & sources

Palm Beach County's Building Division says all of unincorporated Palm Beach County is within the wind-borne debris region, and its product-approval guidance notes that all of Palm Beach County is classified a wind-borne debris region. That is the zone where the building code requires protected openings, one of the features a wind mitigation report records. Ask your city's building department to confirm for your address.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2024 1-year, tables B25034/B25035, via Census Reporter; Palm Beach County PZB, Wind Speeds; Palm Beach County PBO-118 (product approval) (checked Oct 21, 2026).

North Palm Beach wind mitigation, 4-point & roof certification inspectors

North Palm Beach has some of the oldest housing in the north county. The median home was built in 1972, and almost three in four (73%) of its roughly 8,400 units predate 1980. Condos are a big part of the picture: 45% of homes are in buildings with 20 or more units. If you own one, ask your agent which reports a condo unit policy needs before you book anything. If you own a house, a 4-point inspection is close to a given for Citizens applicants, and a roof inspection follows once a shingle roof passes 25 years, or tile or metal passes 50.

Wind-borne debris region & sources

Palm Beach County's Building Division says all of unincorporated Palm Beach County is within the wind-borne debris region, and its product-approval guidance notes that all of Palm Beach County is classified a wind-borne debris region. That is the zone where the building code requires protected openings, one of the features a wind mitigation report records. Ask your city's building department to confirm for your address.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2024 5-year, tables B25034/B25035/B25024/B25003, via Census Reporter; Palm Beach County PZB, Wind Speeds; Palm Beach County PBO-118 (product approval) (checked Oct 21, 2026).

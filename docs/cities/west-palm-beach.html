West Palm Beach wind mitigation, 4-point & roof certification inspectors

West Palm Beach has a lot of older housing, from 1920s bungalows in Flamingo Park and Grandview Heights to mid-century ranch homes, so many insurers ask for 4-point and roof documentation. Newer condos and townhomes often benefit most from a wind mitigation report.

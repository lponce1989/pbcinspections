Jupiter wind mitigation, 4-point & roof certification inspectors

Jupiter has about 31,257 housing units. Their median year built is 1989, and about 15% were built before 1980 (ACS 2024 5-year estimates). A home built in 1989 is 37 years old in 2026, past the 20-year age at which Citizens requires a 4-point inspection.

Palm Beach County's Building Division says all of unincorporated Palm Beach County is within the wind-borne debris region, and its product-approval guidance notes that all of Palm Beach County is classified a wind-borne debris region. That is the zone where the building code requires protected openings, one of the features a wind mitigation report records. Ask your city's building department to confirm for your address.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2024 5-year, tables B25034/B25035, via Census Reporter; Palm Beach County PZB, Wind Speeds; Palm Beach County PBO-118 (product approval) (checked 2026-10-21).

About & disclaimer

Palm Beach Inspection Finder is an independent directory of Palm Beach County, Florida inspectors who do wind mitigation, 4-point, and roof certification reports. It is owned by Leonard Ponce.

How listings work

Each listing comes from the inspector's own public website. Each one shows a source link and the date we checked it. If an inspector doesn't publish a price, we show "call for price". Listings are not endorsements.

Who we include

  • The business offers wind mitigation, 4-point, or roof certification inspections.
  • Its own website says it serves the city it is listed under (or all of Palm Beach County or Florida).
  • It has a working public website we can cite.
  • Listing is free. Nobody pays to be included or to be ranked; listings appear in no particular order.

Removal and corrections

  • We remove a listing if the business asks us to, if its website stops working, or if it stops offering these inspections.
  • We correct prices, phone numbers, service areas or license details when the business or a reader shows us a public source. Email corrections@pbcinspections.com.
  • "Local (PBC-based)" means the business states a Palm Beach County address on its site. "Address not stated" means we did not find one.

Licenses

Where shown, license types and numbers come from the Florida DBPR license search on the date listed. "Not verified" means we could not confirm a license from official sources, not that the business is unlicensed. Check one yourself.

Affiliate & referral disclosure

Right now we have no paid relationships with any inspector, insurer, insurance agency, or lead-generation company. No listing is paid for, and nothing on this site is an affiliate link. In the future the site may earn referral fees, for example from an insurance-quote tool or from featured listings. If that happens we will update this page first, name the type of relationship, and clearly label any paid or affiliate link or placement where it appears. Payment will never change the prices or license information we show.

Not insurance advice

Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, change, or cancel an insurance policy. We do not sell insurance and we are not licensed as an insurance agent. Whether an inspection earns a discount, and how much, depends on your insurer's filed rates and on what the inspector verifies. Talk to a licensed insurance agent or your insurer before you make coverage decisions.

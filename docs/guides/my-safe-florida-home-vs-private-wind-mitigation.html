My Safe Florida Home inspection vs. a private wind mitigation

Not insurance advice. General information only. Your insurer or agent decides what it needs.

Status checked 2026-10-21: the My Safe Florida Home (MSFH) program's website says it offers free wind mitigation inspections and grants of up to $10,000, and asks homeowners to create an account and complete a Prioritization Questionnaire. Applicants are then placed in inspection groups (1-5) and can apply when their group's window opens. Funding is set by the Legislature, so check mysafeflhome.com before you plan around it.

Which should you choose?

If you need a form for your insurer soon, a private inspection is usually faster. If you plan to strengthen your home and may qualify for a grant, the MSFH route is how you get there. Ask the program whether its report can also go to your insurer.

FAQ

Is My Safe Florida Home accepting applications?

When we checked on 2026-10-21, the program's site asked homeowners to create an account and complete a Prioritization Questionnaire to be placed in an inspection group. Funding depends on the Legislature; check mysafeflhome.com.

Is the My Safe Florida Home inspection free?

Yes, for eligible homeowners, according to the program.

Do I need the program inspection to get an MSFH grant?

Yes. Grant applications follow the program's own initial inspection.

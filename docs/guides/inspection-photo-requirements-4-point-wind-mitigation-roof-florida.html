Inspection photos: what the 4-point, roof and wind mitigation forms require

Checked Nov 12, 2026

Not insurance advice. General information only. Your insurer or agent decides what it needs.

Short answer: Citizens' sample 4-point form says photos must accompany each form and lists the minimum: each side of the home, each roof slope, the water heater and plumbing, the open electrical panel and the box with the panel off, and every hazard noted. Its Roof Inspection Form needs photos of each roof slope and every hazard. The wind mitigation form, OIR-B1-1802 (Rev. 04/26), says at least one photograph or document providing proof must accompany it for each attribute marked in questions 2 through 9.

Photos on a 4-point inspection

Citizens' sample 4-Point Inspection Form (Insp4pt 03 25) says: "Photos must accompany each 4-Point Inspection Form." Its list of minimum photo requirements is:

  • Dwelling: each side
  • Roof: each slope
  • Plumbing: the water heater, including its temperature and pressure relief valve, under-cabinet plumbing and drains, and exposed valves
  • The main electrical service panel, open, with its interior door label
  • The electrical box with the panel off
  • All hazards or deficiencies noted in the report

The HVAC section of the same form also asks the inspector to attach photos of the HVAC equipment, including the dated manufacturer's plate. Citizens says the sample form itself is not required, but any other inspection report submitted for consideration must include at least the same level of detail to be acceptable. See what the 4-point form flags for the electrical, plumbing and HVAC items those photos document.

Photos for a roof inspection

Citizens' sample Roof Inspection Form (RCF-1 03 25) says photos of each roof slope showing the roof's condition must be submitted with the form. Its minimum photo requirements are each roof slope and all hazards or deficiencies.

The roof section of the 4-point form says that with photos of each roof slope, it can take the place of the Roof Inspection Form. Citizens' answer on the same question says the 4-point form can be submitted in lieu of the RCF-1 if photos of each side of the roof are provided and the form is signed and dated by a verifiable, Florida-licensed professional. More in what's on the RCF-1.

Photos on the wind mitigation form

The note at the top of the OIR-B1-1802 (Rev. 04/26) says any documentation used in validating the compliance or existence of each construction or mitigation attribute must accompany the form, and that at least one photograph or document providing proof must accompany it to validate each attribute marked in questions 2 through 9. Those questions on the form are:

  • 2. Region (design wind speed)
  • 3. Roof slope
  • 4. Roof covering
  • 5. Roof deck attachment
  • 6. Roof-to-wall attachment
  • 7. Roof geometry
  • 8. Sealed roof deck / secondary water resistance
  • 9. Opening protection

The same note says a FORTIFIED Home Roof, Silver or Gold certificate may be used to validate the attributes marked in questions 4 through 6 and 8 through 9, with a photocopy attached, and that the insurer may ask additional questions about the features verified on the form. See what a FORTIFIED certificate can cover and the form question by question.

What Citizens checks before giving credits

Citizens' Wind Loss Mitigation Guide for the Rev. 04/26 form lists this as a key change: "Previously only photographs were allowed; now photographs OR valid documentation may be accepted, such as Fortified Home certificates or compliance documentation." The guide says attributes 2 through 9 must be accompanied by at least one photograph to obtain credit, unless additional compliance documentation is provided, and that Underwriting may ask for additional photographs or other documentation.

The guide adds feature-specific notes:

  • Roof-to-wall wraps: single and double wraps require photographs even if a FORTIFIED Home Gold certificate is available. See toe nails, clips and wraps.
  • Hip roof: photographs are required for the hip roof discount. If you are applying only for a hip roof credit, the guide says clear photographs can be submitted instead of a full inspection. See roof shape on the form.
  • Secondary water resistance: because the secondary water barrier is generally not visible on a completed roof, the guide says the inspection should include photos, permits, a FORTIFIED Home certificate or other supporting documentation before a sealed roof deck is selected. See what counts as a sealed roof deck.

Before credits are requested, the guide's checklist says to verify that the required photos for attributes 2 through 9 are complete and that required product approval certificates, permits or other documentation are included. If something appears to be missing, inaccurate or inconsistent, it says to contact the inspector to correct it before submitting to Citizens.

Paperwork that backs up the photos

Citizens says to give your inspector copies of any mitigation documents before the inspection begins, because documentation helps you receive discounts for features that may not be clearly marked or easily visible. Its examples are receipts or other documents with product specifications, work orders and invoices, roofing permits, and notice-of-acceptance letters for mitigation products, which it says usually come from installers or manufacturers.

Documentation matters most for shutters and windows. On the OIR-B1-1802, level N covers opening protection systems that appear to meet level A or B but have no documentation of compliance. See how the form rates windows and shutters, and for permits, how to find your roof and window permits in Palm Beach County.

Before you book

  • Clear the attic access: Citizens says attic access is required to verify certain mitigation features, and if the inspector cannot get into the attic you may not be eligible for discounts for roof-to-wall attachment, roof-deck attachment and secondary water resistance. More in how to prepare for a wind mitigation inspection.
  • Check the date: Citizens says a four-point inspection must have been completed within the last year by a verifiable, Florida-licensed inspector.
  • Compare bundle prices: 8 of the 16 companies we list publish a 4-point + wind mitigation bundle price. FAQ

What photos does a 4-point inspection need?

Citizens' sample 4-point form lists each side of the home, each roof slope, the water heater with its relief valve, under-cabinet plumbing and drains, exposed valves, the open main electrical panel with its interior door label, the electrical box with the panel off, and all hazards or deficiencies. The HVAC section also asks for photos of the equipment with its dated manufacturer's plate.

Can a 4-point inspection replace the roof certification?

Citizens says the 4-point form can be submitted in lieu of the Roof Inspection Form (RCF-1) if photos of each side of the roof are provided and the form is signed and dated by a verifiable, Florida-licensed professional.

Does the wind mitigation form require photos?

The OIR-B1-1802 (Rev. 04/26) says at least one photograph or document providing proof must accompany the form to validate each attribute marked in questions 2 through 9. A FORTIFIED Home certificate may be used to validate questions 4 through 6 and 8 through 9.

Can I get a hip roof credit from photos alone?

Citizens' Wind Loss Mitigation Guide says that if you are applying only for a hip roof credit, clear photographs can be submitted instead of a full inspection.

How long a wind mitigation, 4-point or roof report stays valid

Not insurance advice. General information only. Your insurer or agent decides what it needs.

ReportHow long it's acceptedSource
Wind mitigation (OIR-B1-1802)Up to 5 years, if no material changes to the structure and no inaccuracies on the formFlorida OIR; Florida DFS
4-pointCitizens: dated within 12 months before a new-business application (shorter for some lender-placed and prior-sinkhole cases)Citizens

Wind mitigation: up to five years

OIR: the form "is valid for up to five (5) years provided no material changes are made to the structure or inaccuracies are found on the form." The DFS says the same. If you replace the roof or add impact windows or shutters, a new inspection can record the new features.

4-point: 12 months for Citizens

Citizens' support center: "The four-point inspection report must be dated within the last 12 months prior to the new-business application submission date." Exceptions: lender-placed coverage in effect within 45 days of the effective date needs an inspection within 10 days before it; risks with a prior sinkhole claim need one within 90 days. Private insurers set their own rules.

Roof certification

Citizens requires roof documentation for shingle roofs over 25 years and tile/metal roofs over 50 years, showing at least five years of remaining useful life. Citizens also accepts a 4-point form in place of the Roof Inspection Form if it includes photos of each side of the roof and is signed by a verifiable Florida-licensed professional, subject to the same 12-month 4-point rule.

FAQ

How long is a Florida wind mitigation report valid?

Up to five years, if there are no material changes to the structure and no inaccuracies on the form, according to the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation.

How old can a 4-point inspection be for Citizens?

Citizens says it must be dated within the last 12 months before the new-business application submission date, with shorter windows in some cases.

How long is a roof certification good for?

We did not find a separate published age limit from Citizens. Ask your agent what your insurer accepts.

4-point vs. wind mitigation vs. roof certification: which one your insurer wants

4-point inspection

Citizens Property Insurance says it "requires a four-point inspection for all property owner, dwelling and mobile home applications for properties more than 20 years old." A Florida-licensed inspector reports the age, type, and condition of the electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and roof. Citizens says serious hazards should be fixed before you apply. Private insurers set their own age cutoffs.

Wind mitigation (OIR-B1-1802)

The Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection Form, OIR-B1-1802, is adopted under Rule 69O-170.0155, F.A.C. and section 627.0629, Florida Statutes. Florida OIR revised it effective April 1, 2026 (Rev. 04/26). According to OIR, a completed form "is valid for up to five (5) years provided no material changes are made to the structure or inaccuracies are found on the form." Citizens says wind mitigation inspections document features that may qualify a home for premium discounts.

Roof certification

Citizens says roofs older than 25 years (soft, such as shingle) or 50 years (hard, such as tile, slate, clay, concrete, or metal) need documentation showing at least five years of remaining useful life. A Florida-licensed inspector completes Citizens' Roof Inspection Form. Other insurers may use different ages and forms.

Can I get them in one visit?

Often, yes. Many Palm Beach County inspectors sell a 4-point + wind mitigation bundle. See the Boca Raton listings.
